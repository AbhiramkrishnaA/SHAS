About the society

Cooperation for
lasting wellbeing.

Registered in 2006, the State Agri Horticultural Development Co-operative Society Ltd. is a Class I cooperative accredited by the Government of Kerala.

2,783 membersA farmer-led society working across agriculture, horticulture, livelihoods and green infrastructure.
Our purpose

All-round empowerment and wellbeing of agri-horticultural farmers through cooperation.

Connecting cooperative action with practical development for farmers and communities.

Agriculture and livelihoods

Cooperative finance, agri trade, livestock development, women’s employment support and nursery entrepreneurship.

Green infrastructure

Landscape design, horticultural development, civil works and urban beautification through SAHS Landscape Engineering Division.

Environment and community

Environmental restoration, useful plant propagation, eco-friendly gardening materials and consultancy for institutions and communities.

Registered under the Kerala Co-operative Societies Act, 1969. Registered office: Karshika Bhavan, Vedivechancoil P.O., Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala 695501.

Leadership

Mr. Binu Pilot

Founder & Chief Promoter
Chairman & Managing Director

Landscape professional · Horticulturist · Environmental advocate · Social entrepreneur

Ideas become lasting change when people can build on them.

“Transforming ideas into institutions, knowledge into opportunities, and landscapes into sustainable environments.”

Mr. Binu Pilot’s work brings together computer literacy, landscape design, horticulture, environmental sustainability and cooperative development. He founded the Computer Literacy Mission of India with a vision of taking digital education into communities. Its profile reports that between 2005 and 2010, the mission facilitated 149 learning centres across Kerala and trained more than 500,000 people.

In 2006, he founded SAHS to connect agriculture and horticulture with landscape development, urban greening and cooperative initiatives. His professional work includes more than 1,500 landscape and horticultural projects in India and abroad. SAHS reports completing over 100 landscape development, urban beautification and green development projects across Kerala.

Professional background and education

His qualifications include an M.Sc. in Landscape Studies, an MBA, postgraduate training in Landscape and Ornamental Gardening, and diplomas in Cooperative Management, Horticulture Nursery Management and Agricultural Science. He also holds a Certificate in Landscape Architecture and Planning from IIT and has completed short-term courses in agriculture, horticulture, environmental science and ecology.
